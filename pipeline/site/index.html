HaloAI

A clip-on e-ink voice assistant that never acts without your press.

Sample data: nothing real is created or sent.

  1. Speak. Hold the badge button and talk. NVIDIA Parakeet turns it into text on your phone; only text leaves it. Here, you type.
  2. Propose. NVIDIA Nemotron 3 Ultra on Nebius Token Factory reads your calendar, contacts, memory and the web (Tavily), and proposes one action.
  3. Review. The e-ink screen shows exactly what will happen.
  4. Press. Nothing is created or sent until you press. A double press cancels.
